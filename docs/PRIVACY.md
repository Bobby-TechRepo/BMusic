# BMusic privacy policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

BMusic is a music player for the audio files stored on your device. It is built so that your
data stays with you.

## What BMusic does not do

- It does not collect, sell or share personal data.
- It has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking.
- It does not ask for internet access, so it cannot send anything over the network.

## What BMusic uses on your device

| Access | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Music and audio (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`, or storage on Android 12 and older) | To find and play your audio files, and to read their tags (titles, artwork, lyrics, volume-levelling values). |
| Notifications | To show playback controls while music plays. |
| Foreground media playback | To keep playing when the app is in the background. |

## What BMusic stores, and where

All of the following stays on your device, in the app's private storage:

- your settings, equalizer and sound-effect levels;
- playlists, favourites, play counts and listening history;
- lyrics you import;
- the last crash report, if the app ever crashes.

You can delete all of it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

## Backups

- **Android backup:** if it is turned on for your device, Android may include your settings,
  playlists, favourites, history and imported lyrics in your device backup. That backup is
  handled by Google under your Google account settings; BMusic does not see or control it. The
  current playing queue and crash reports are left out.
- **Backup to a file:** "Back up" in Settings writes a file only to the place you choose.

## Crash reports

If BMusic crashes, a short technical report is kept on your device. It contains the app version,
the phone model and Android version, and the error details; it contains no personal data or file
names. It is only sent if you tap **Send crash report** in Settings and choose an app and
recipient yourself.

## Children

BMusic does not collect data from anyone, including children.
