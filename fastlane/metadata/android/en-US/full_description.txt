BMusic plays the music stored on your phone, and only that: no account, no ads, no tracking and no internet connection needed.

LISTEN THE WAY YOU LIKE
• Gapless playback and smooth fades between songs
• Volume levelling with ReplayGain, per song or per album
• Equalizer with presets, plus bass boost, surround and loudness knobs
• Playback speed from 0.5× to 2×, remembered between sessions
• Sleep timer that fades out gently, or stops at the end of the song

LYRICS
• Synced lyrics that follow the song; tap a line to jump to it
• Reads lyrics stored in MP3, FLAC, Ogg, Opus and M4A files
• Import .lrc files for any song

YOUR LIBRARY, ORGANISED
• Fast search across songs, albums, artists and playlists
• Playlists you can reorder, plus Favourites, Recently added, Most played, Rediscover and Never played
• Import and export M3U playlists to move them between apps and computers

KNOW YOUR LISTENING
• Listening time, plays, top songs and top artists for 7 days, 30 days or all time
• An activity chart of when you listen

EVERYWHERE YOU LISTEN
• Home-screen widget
• Lock screen and notification controls with favourite and shuffle buttons
• Android Auto
• Resume from the system media controls, even after a restart

SAFE WITH YOUR DATA
• Back up favourites, playlists, play counts, history and lyrics to a file, and restore them on any phone
• Nothing leaves your phone unless you share it

Available in English, Spanish, French, Japanese and Chinese.
